Book Published: Platform Engineering and Security

June 20, 2026 3 min read Govindarajan Vishnuchithan

Quick takeaway: Practical templates and checklists accelerate platform adoption and reduce organizational friction.

In June 2026, a new book was published that explores the intersection of platform engineering, cloud delivery, and security practices.

The book distills operational patterns, governance models, and practical examples for building platform capabilities that scale. Chapters include platform operating models, developer onboarding, and secure delivery patterns with actionable checklists.

What readers will learn

  • How to align platform engineering with business outcomes and developer needs.
  • How to create secure delivery models that support rapid innovation.
  • How to structure platform teams, governance, and golden paths to reduce friction.

Book themes

This publication focuses on practical approaches rather than theory. It emphasizes how platform engineering can deliver measurable developer velocity, operational resilience, and security maturity.

Why the book matters

Many engineering organizations invest in platform tools without a clear adoption path. This book helps readers define the right platform structure and make security a natural part of delivery.
